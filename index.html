    Art Lab

    7 experiments

    Flow fields, shaders and particle systems: the generative side of the lab. Most of them take your input and export what you make.

    • Blossoming Flowers

      Petals unfold in a loop of pure CSS animation.

      CSSAnimation

    • Flow Fields

      Particles drift through an invisible vector field. Press D, or double-tap, to see it.

      CanvasParticles

    • Text Fields

      Type becomes a particle system that flows through the field.

      TypographyCanvas

    • Experimental Math

      Equations plotted as motion, one vector at a time.

      MathGenerative

    • Adaptive Fields

      Tune color, noise and density and export the result.

      Interactivep5.js

    • Liquid Shapes

      A fragment shader you can steer in real time, with video export.

      WebGLShader

    • Particular Drift

      Any image dissolves into tens of thousands of drifting particles.

      WebGLParticles
