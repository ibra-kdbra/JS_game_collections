Shadow Dog Runner

Roll through the creatures of the night. Touching them any other way costs a life.

  • ←→ run, ↑ jump, ↓ sit / dive
  • Enter or Space (hold) roll: the only way to hit enemies safely
  • Roll + ↑ to jump while rolling, ↓ in the air to dive
  • P / Esc pause, R restart

Use the arrows to run, jump and dive. Hold Roll to attack.
