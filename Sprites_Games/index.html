Sprite Lab

Eight short lessons in how 2D games move: cutting frames from a sprite sheet, scrolling parallax layers, enemy movement patterns, collisions and a state machine. They follow the tutorials by Franks Laboratory, whose code and sprite art they are built from. Each one runs on its own page and fits a phone screen.

Lessons

  1. 01 Sprite animation One sprite sheet, ten animations. Pick one from the menu and watch its frames step. MenuOpen
  2. 02 Parallax background Five layers scroll at different speeds to fake depth. The slider sets the game speed. SliderOpen
  3. 03 Enemy movement A swarm of enemies, each picking a new random target and easing towards it. WatchOpen
  4. 04 Collision detection Tap or click the board for an explosion: sprite frames, sound and rotation. TapOpen
  5. 05 Point and shoot Shoot the ravens before one gets past you. Hits are found by colour on a hidden canvas. TapOpen
  6. 06 Enemy types Worms, ghosts and spiders: one base class, three ways to move. WatchOpen
  7. 07 Side scroller Jump the enemies for points. Arrow keys on a keyboard; swipe up to jump on a phone. KeysSwipeOpen
  8. 08 State management A dog driven by a state machine: stand, sit, run, jump and fall, from the arrow keys or the on-screen pad. KeysPadOpen
