    Settings

    Swipes always steer on touch screens. The d-pad is there if you prefer buttons. Motion follows your system setting unless you pick one.

    How to play

    Steer

    Arrow keys or WASD. Two turns can be queued, so a quick double tap makes a tight U.

    On a phone, swipe anywhere. Each swipe is one turn; keep your finger down and swipe again to chain turns. The snake never reverses into itself.

    P or Esc pause · M mute · R replay after a crash.

    On the board

    • Food: +1 length, 10 points.
    • Golden bonus: +3 length, 50 points. Its ring is a timer.
    • Speed-up for 6 s. Food scores double while it lasts.
    • Slow-down for 6 s.
    • Portal: enter one, leave from its twin, same direction.
    • Solid edges kill; dashed edges wrap.
