How to play

One button

Tap, click or press Space to flip gravity. The courier falls to the other rail and keeps running. You can flip only from a rail; a tap just before landing is kept and flips you on touchdown.

The run speeds up the further you get, and the gaps between obstacles shrink. New obstacles join at 120, 220, 420, 700, 950, 1250 and 1700 m.

P or Esc pause · M mute. The run pauses by itself when you switch tabs.

In the corridor

  • Spikes and pylons: be on the other rail.
  • Holes: a rail with red caps has no floor. Don't run into them.
  • Drones patrol up and down their dotted track. Double-ringed ones reach the rails.
  • Shutters burn one half at a time, then pause while the other half charges.
  • Volt shards: 25 points × multiplier. Eight in a row (or close calls) raise it, up to ×5. Missing a row lowers it.
  • Shield: takes one hit or throws you out of a hole. Resets the multiplier.
  • Magnet: pulls nearby shards in for 7 s.
