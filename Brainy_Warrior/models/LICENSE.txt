Character models in this folder
===============================

warrior.glb, wizard.glb
    From "RPG Characters - Nov 2020" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com).
orc.glb, orc-skull.glb, tribal.glb
    From "Ultimate Monsters" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com).

Both packs are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The files were converted with Brainy_Warrior/tools/pack-models.py (meshes and
props merged into one skinned mesh per character, textures atlased and resized,
unused animation clips removed) and then simplified and compressed with
gltfpack from meshoptimizer (https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer, MIT). The
wizard's hat is not in these files: the game adds it in code (js/actors.js).

The original licence text shipped with both packs reads:

    LowPoly Models by @Quaternius
    License: CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
