How to play

  • Slide. Each move sends the warrior in a straight line until something stops him.
  • Defeat every enemy. Slide into an enemy to defeat it. You stop on its tile.
  • Mind the sea. Rocks stop you on the tile in front of them. With nothing to stop you, you fall in and lose.
  • Use the wizard. He blocks you like a rock. Magic sends him to his next glowing circle (one move).
  • Think ahead. Every level has an optimal solution. Match it for three stars.
