How to play: Swipe, or use the arrow keys (or WASD), to slide the tiles. Two equal tiles merge into one. U undoes a move (3 per game), ? asks the AI for a hint, R restarts. Switch to Watch AI to see the AI play; games the AI moves in don't count towards your best score.
