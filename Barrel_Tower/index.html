The Ramps

Climb to the beacon.

Game Lab arcade

BARRELTOWER

A scrap-metal brute has taken the half-built tower. Climb it anyway.

How to play

  • Move ← → or A D. Climb ↑ ↓ or W S at a ladder.
  • Jump Space, Z or K. You can't steer in the air, and a long drop is fatal.
  • Hammer jump to grab one: it smashes barrels and sparks for ten seconds, but you can't climb while you hold it.
  • Broken ladders are only for barrels and sparks.
  • Bonus counts down: what's left is added when you clear a stage. At zero you lose a life.
  • Pause P or Esc. Mute M.

Stages

  • The Ramps barrels roll down six girders and sometimes take a ladder. Jump them for 100 points. The ones that reach the fuel drum set it alight, and the sparks come after you.
  • The Lifts ride the elevators up, hop across, and dodge the springs that bounce along the top.
  • The Rivets walk over all eight rivets to pull them. Pull the last one and the brute comes down with the tower.

Every round brings faster barrels, more sparks and quicker lifts. An extra life at 10,000 points.
