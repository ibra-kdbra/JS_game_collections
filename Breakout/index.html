How to play

  • Move drag anywhere (the paddle follows your finger's motion, so your thumb stays clear of the ball), move the mouse, or use ← → / A D.
  • Launch tap, click, or press Space.
  • Aim the ball leaves at an angle set by where it meets the paddle: centre goes up, edges go wide.
  • Combo every 4 bricks without touching the paddle raises the multiplier, up to ×6.
  • Pause P or Esc. Mute M.
