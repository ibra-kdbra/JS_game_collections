Opening the deck

Three ways into the Art Lab, each built around opening a deck of cards. All three use the real seven pieces: tap Enter on any card to open the piece in a new tab. Sound is off by default; every prototype has a replay button.

Booster Pack

Tear a sealed foil pack along its top edge; seven cards burst into a fan and you flip each one to reveal it, with holo pulls for the WebGL pieces.

Dealer's Table

Card Box
