Tanks

How to play

  • Feed. Click the water to drop food ($5). Hungry fish turn green and open their mouths. Starving ones tremble and show a !.
  • Grow. Guppies grow as they eat. Medium ones drop silver coins ($15), big ones gold ($35).
  • Collect. Click coins before they fade on the sand.
  • Aliens. A siren warns you first. While an alien is in the tank, clicks zap it instead of feeding. Beat it for a treasure chest ($1,500).
  • Egg. Buy the three egg pieces to clear the tank. The egg hatches a pet that helps you in later tanks.

Shop (along the top)

  • Guppy $100 · Food better food fills fish up for longer · More food drop more at once.
  • Carnivore $1,000: eats baby guppies, drops diamonds ($200) · Breeder $750: has baby guppies while fed.
  • Zapper hits aliens harder.

Keys: 1–7 buy from the shop · P pause · M mute.
