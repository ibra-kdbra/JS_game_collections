How to play

  • Feed click or tap the water to drop food (5 coins). Only fish that are hungry eat, and a hungry fish turns pale green and opens its mouth. Leave it too long and it dies.
  • Grow a minnow grows as it eats: medium minnows drop silver coins (15), large ones gold (35).
  • Collect click coins before they fade on the sand. A crab will pick them up for you.
  • Invaders a warning sounds first. While one is in the tank, clicks are shots: hit it until it gives up, and it leaves a treasure chest (1,500).
  • Clear the tank by buying all three pearls.
