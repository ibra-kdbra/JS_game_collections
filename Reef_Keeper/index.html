How to play

  • Feed. Click the water to drop food ($5). Hungry fish turn olive and open their mouths. Starving ones tremble and show a !.
  • Grow. Guppies grow as they eat. Medium ones drop silver coins ($15), big ones gold ($35).
  • Collect. Click coins before they fade on the sand.
  • Aliens. A siren warns you first. While an alien is in the tank, clicks zap it. Some aliens need other tricks: each one is explained the first time you meet it.
  • Egg. Buy the three egg pieces to clear the level. Some eggs hatch pets that help you later.
  • Stars. Clear a level quickly for up to three stars.

Keys: 1–9 buy from the shop · P pause · M mute.
