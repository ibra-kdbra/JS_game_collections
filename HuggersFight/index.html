Huggers Fight

A fighter game by ibra-kdbra

Wipe out every enemy outpost on the planet. Almost everything breaks, some things burn.

  • WASD / Arrows move, jump, climb
  • Z / left click shoot · X / middle click roll · C / right click grenade
  • P / Esc pause · R restart · up to 4 gamepads for co-op

D-pad to move and climb. Roll puts out fire and does melee damage. Grenade jumps reach high places.
